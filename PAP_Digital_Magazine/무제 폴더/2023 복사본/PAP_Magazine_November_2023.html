I SEE I(S)

I SEE I(s) is a narrative editorial series about a self identification journey based on an important friend of ours. We created these expressive tho emotion underlined imageries walking through He/She or theirs transformational voyage of self-discovering in which we observed, took part and supported.   Nowadays, we see that gender fluidity has become a more and more discussed and acceptable concept throughout the younger generations. With the excessive use of social media, this ideology travels, evolves and being practiced inordinately by underaged youths. It is certainly great to have more freedom of being whoever we want to be, nevertheless it rises the question that whether underaged youths have sufficient knowledges or experiences to ‘decide’ on their gender identities, or if it has become a ‘trend’ to put a ‘label’ on oneself?   Our friend who inspired us for this project, went through phases in which they felt like not belonging to any of the existing gender group, then gradually started to embrace their femininity and identified themselves as a trans girl while trying whatever they can to ‘become’ a more feminine girl. Just before they decide to get the physical transforming surgery, they realised that they might have gone too far without actually acknowledging their action, confused and terrified of the influence they got from social media and finally started to really see themselves without worrying who they are or where they belong.   We captured these complex stages with an underlining emotional transformation of experimenting, celebrating, confirming, confusing, realising, reassuring and releasing. By creating a twin characters to better explain the complexity of gender expression and self-discovering journey that we would like to share and encourage those who are experiencing their changes.

예상치 못한 것을 포착하는 시선으로 촬영된 'I see I(s)'은(는) 하나의 무드에 안주하기를 거부한다. molamolangela, _.iiimiii._, sultryvirgin_official의 피스들이 공통된 실로 기능한다.

ATLANTIS

Long before cell phones were invented and AI endangered our jobs, long before bed bugs infested Paris, and long before the question, whether or not Ye (formally known as Kanye West) could become U.S. president even arose. There was a time when all of earth’s creatures coexisted in harmony. A time with endless resources, when materialism wasn’t valued higher than morals and everyone was free, regardless of identity and origin. That was the age of peace...the age of Atlantis. Today we know, that Atlantis probably never existed. But the myth stays – carrying with it the idea of a utopian world. Together, the Ukrainian photographer Sasha Kushakov, upcoming stylist Amelie Bachert and the successful Hair and Makeup Artist Servulo Mendez Rey formed a team to make that dream become reality: The Editorial “Atlantis” features a selection of international models from Senegal, Vietnam, the UK and Japan who are staged as priests and priestesses of the ancient city. For that, the stylist decided to not only work with established German designers like Judith Bondy and Olivia Ballard, but also with Newcomer-talents such as Lea Theres Lahr-Thiele and Laurin Schuler. The team’s vision was to intentionally avoid name dropping and shallowness – but rather to connect creative minds from all over the world. DREAM MORPHING

"Dream Morphing" is an innovative photographic project brought to life by our creative team. The project combines the realms of real-world photography and cutting-edge artificial intelligence technology to seamlessly blend the boundaries between dreams and reality. The core concept behind this endeavor is the translation of various types of dreams into vivid visual narratives, spanning the spectrum from delightful fantasies to the enigmatic phase that exists between slumber and wakefulness, and even delving into the eerie depths of nightmares. In our interpretation of a "Pleasant Fantasy," we capture images that exude a carefree and vibrant atmosphere, evoking the essence of a whimsical fairytale. These photographs transport viewers to a world where the mundane gives way to the extraordinary, painting scenes filled with enchantment and wonder. The "Intermediary Phase" between dream and wakefulness presents a unique challenge, as it is a realm where reality is still intertwined with the surreal. In this segment of our project, we aim to depict images that are dreamlike but grounded in elements of reality. Here, viewers will find themselves in a twilight zone where the ordinary is infused with elements of the extraordinary, leaving them in a state of fascination and contemplation. Lastly, our exploration of the "Nightmare" realm delves into the sinister and unsettling aspects of dreams. These photographs are characterized by dark and foreboding imagery, unsettling visuals, and an atmosphere that evokes fear and discomfort. In this segment, we confront the viewer with the unsettling and disturbing, inviting them to peer into the shadows of the human subconscious. "Dream Morphing" harnesses the power of photography and AI to offer viewers a captivating journey through the diverse landscapes of dreams. It is an exploration of the human psyche, where the boundaries between the real and the surreal are blurred, and where emotions, sensations, and the fantastical aspects of our innermost thoughts come to life through the lens of art and technology. This project serves as an artistic portal to the enigmatic world of dreams, inviting all to ponder the intricate tapestry of the mind and the limitless possibilities that exist within the realm of imagination.
