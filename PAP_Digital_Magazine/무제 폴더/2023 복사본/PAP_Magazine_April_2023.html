FIGHTING FOR YOURSELF

This project represents a significant period of transformation where you deconstructed everything to rebuild your world and redefine your unique identity. At this point, closing one book and starting another is such an important part of the journey. By shedding your old beliefs and unlearning what you thought grounded you. You were able to construct a new and improved version of yourself. The aftermath of these ups and downs is exemplified by the extraordinary power that allows you to mold and shape your identity into an authentic representation of your true self, even if it may have once seemed unattainable. This power is truly magnificent and serves as a reminder that you have the ability to redesign and refine your sense of self in the face of adversity. It’s a testament to your resilience and unwavering commitment to personal growth and development. Welcome to the battle of your freedom. I have chosen to capture the intricate nature of the identity, tracing its evolution and the emotions that come with it. My aim is to spotlight the freedom it brings, while recognizing the significance of difficult decisions such as revealing one's true self or making major life choices. Each individual's unique life experiences and personalities reveal the beauty of their identity and journey. It's a representation of the many battles one may face in order to achieve a harmonious and radiant unity. The introspection involved in this process is both beautiful and requires great strength to become the person you meant to be and take pride in it. I aspire to represent personal identity in a meaningful way, encompassing all the nuances that make an individual unique and distinct from others.

LORELEI X MONET

Meeting Lorelei by Monet’s Waterlily Pond Within German folklore, the figure of Lorelei, a beautiful maiden, threw herself into the Rhine River in despair over a faithless lover and was then transformed into a siren. Her story paints her as both a tragic and fragile character grieving a loss of love, as well as being a malicious enchantress set on beckoning sailors to their death, weaponising her beauty and alluring songs. These photos explore the duality of Lorelei‘s mythical figure within the impressionist wonderland of Monet’s ethereal Water Lilies series of paintings, playing to both sides of Lorelei’s character. Will the waterlilies enhance Lorelei’s beauty or will this temptress who once lost faith in love reveal her true self and spill blood in the pond? It will remain a mystery again.
