APOTROPAICO

Apotropaico is a visual fantasy finding magic forces that serve as a defence against evil, protecting from hurt, harm, sadness. As in a sort of modern tarot, the images are thought to have cathartic and purifying influences. As individuals and within societies, we preserve belief in the face of uncertainty and mystery of life. I want to imagine and create visions that awake our sense of magical powers that can protect us from the bad influences that feel extremely present in the shaken reality of our present. As in traditional superstition, elements such as mirrors or shiny objects are present to deflect evil; water and other liquids remind of libations to the gods; and gestures as sticking out the tongue, or deformed faces are meant to scare evil energies away. Sumptuosity and sensuality are also present, it is the sexual power of change, pleasure and creation that we all hold in our bodies. Plants and flowers hold substances that change our consciousness, in their presence we create ritual and feel reenergised by their beauty and colour.

'Apotropaico'은(는) 다큐멘터리 사진의 엄밀함으로 패션에 접근한다. 이상화도, 은폐도 없다—보통 훨씬 더 무거운 주제에 바치는 정직한 주의력으로 옷을 제시한다.
