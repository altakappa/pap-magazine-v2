QUARRELS ARE EVERYWHERE

This photo shoot will be a surreal, dramatic representation showing that quarrels are everywhere. The shoot will take place in a variety of locations where people with different identities are arguing, with three sets of scenes chosen: in an underground station, on the street and in a park. In order to blur the identities of some of the people arguing, each scene will be chosen to cover one of the persons in the scene with a related element. For example, in the underground station, two people are arguing, one person is yelling at the other to leave, and the other half has already stepped out of the frame. On the street, two people are arguing on the phone at the same time, one housewife pushing a pram, the other looks like a young girl going to a party. In the park, two people arguing, pigeons flying covering the other's face. At the same time, not only during the fight, but also after the fight, for example, the model can be breathing heavily as if she can't catch her breath, touching the tears on her face, leaning against the wall, walking away in the same anger, standing or crouching in a very stagnant way.
