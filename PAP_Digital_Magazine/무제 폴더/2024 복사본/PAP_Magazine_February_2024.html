TAROT FEST

This unique fashion photo series, crafted by the artist using 3D graphics and digital processing, embodies the mysteries of the Major Arcana of the tarot. From the Magician to the World, each of the 5 models skillfully conveys the energy and symbolism of their respective Arcana. The artist draws inspiration not only from aesthetics but also from the idea that tarot can serve as a source of inspiration during times of global crises. The series invites us to glimpse into the abstract world of tarot through the lens of fashion, emphasizing that in these portrayals, one should not merely see individuals but perceive them as unexplored abstractions filled with meaning.
