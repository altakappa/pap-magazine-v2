VIVID DREAMS

Dreams are defined as a sequence of psychic phenomena generated during sleep, which may be partially remembered. However, there are as many dreams as there are nights. Dreams, with their fleeting nature, diversity, and intangibility, are manifestations of the psyche that are difficult to express or share with others. Despite this, they leave us with common sensations ; the distortion of time, the feeling of being lost, of not being in control, or even fear, fear of being hunted, of falling into infinity, of not waking up. These are the shared emotions I aim to capture and convey in this series, entitled Vivid Dreams.

꿈은 수면 중에 생성되어 부분적으로 기억될 수 있는 일련의 정신적 현상으로 정의됩니다. 그러나 밤마다 꿈의 수는 셀 수 없이 많습니다. 꿈은 그 덧없고 다양하며 무형적인 특성 때문에 다른 사람들과 표현하거나 공유하기가 어렵습니다. 그럼에도 불구하고, 꿈은 우리에게 공통된 감각을 남깁니다. 시간의 왜곡, 길을 잃은 느낌, 통제할 수 없는 느낌, 심지어는 두려움까지. 쫓기는 두려움, 끝없는 곳으로 떨어질 것 같은 두려움, 깨어나지 못할 것 같은 두려움 등이 있습니다. 이런 공유된 감정을 이 시리즈 '생생한 꿈들(Vivid Dreams)' 을 통해 포착하고 전달하고자 합니다.

This shooting combines dream fantasy and Tarot elements to depict a story about a person traveling through day and night in a dream. The protagonist saw his own desire in the dream, which turned into a sharp sword that could hurt him at any time. Holding the sword can make him aggressive. If he loses it, he will immediately enter a protective state. This is like the beginning of life which begin an endless cycle from which no one can escape.
