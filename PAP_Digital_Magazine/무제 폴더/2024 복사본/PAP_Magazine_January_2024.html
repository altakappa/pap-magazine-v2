SILENT VIOLENCE

The theme of this photo shoot draws inspiration from campus bullying, depicting two types: physical harm and emotional distress. The female model in this series portrays the physical aspect of campus bullying, such as physical assaults. From her, we can easily discern the visible effects of bullying, yet she remains ignored by the indifferent surroundings. In contrast, the male model, who outwardly appears as a "bully," may be experiencing another form of bullying within the campus, where conformity is demanded with statements like, "You must act this way to fit in with us." How many immature individuals, who may seem like troublemakers, actually find themselves bullied if they don't engage in mistreating others? Campus bullying is a challenging issue that requires identification and attention. Regardless of the type of bullying, we cannot afford to turn a blind eye to it. The photo series aims to shed light on the complexities of campus bullying and urges us not to ignore any form of it, as addressing this problem is crucial for fostering a safe and inclusive educational environment.

'Silent Violence'은(는) 에디토리얼 형식을 뒤집는다. tuuuseew, parvenu_zhang, youwangzhouzhou을(를) 질감이자 텍스트로 사용하며.

THE SKIN I LIVE

Leather pieces are a staple of any wardrobe, be it shoes, jackets, belts, gloves... They are also one of the oldest ways to clothe one self, litterally putting on a second skin. With this fashion and beauty editorial I want to explore the boundaries of leather as it is used today, with a twist on biker, punk and bdsm subcultures. The main focus is on what leather helps hide and what it helps reveal of a person, putting on a piece of something else’s body on to yours, to obtain characteristics of that body and make it our own. Twist that skin into your own, dye it, tear it, sew it together... Using makeup to put on a mask and become another version of yourself, one that you would want the world to see, and thus hiding the intimate parts you want to keep to yourself.  The lighting and art direction is designed to visually represent this aspect of hiding and revealing, by playing with streaks of light, dark shadows .  For the fashion I would love to source vintage leathers, as well as contemporary ones, mixing and matching decades of life on the body. Inspiring fashion enthusiasts to look into their parent’s and grand parent’s closets to find and give new life to old beloved pieces, and rejuvenate their current wardrobe with sustainable fashion.  The makeup in inspired by 90’s and 00’s punk and biker subcultures, giving another nod to that era’s almost forgetten genre’s of fashion within the embrace of the very colorful and fun pop 00’s return to the limelight
