A HAUNTED HOUSE

‘A HAUNTED HOUSE', THIS PROJECT'S MAIN CON- CEPTION, IS A METAPHOR FOR PREJUDICE. A PREJU- DICE MEANS, AS YOU KNOW, AN FIXED IDEA IN YOUR THOUGHT FOR AN OBJECT. IN THIS PROJECT, WE ARE SUPPOSED TO REALIZE VISUALLY AND VIVIDLY TWO WORDS, I.E. 'IN YOUR THOUGHT' AND 'FIXED IDEA' 'HOUSE', ONE OF OUR MAIN FIXTURE, MEANS TO PRIVATE SPACE THAT WE ABSOLUTELY DON'T WANT TO BE BOTHERED OR VIOLATED BY OTHERS. IN THIS POINT WE MIGHT SAY THAT 'HOUSE' IS A ABSTRACT SPACE, WHICH STANDS FOR 'YOUR THOUGHT'. AND WE SOUGHT TO REPRESENT OVERSTATED ILLUSIONS(=FIXED IDEA) IN YOUR THOUGHT BY ANOTHER MAIN FIXTURES, 'AIR-INFLATED-PUP- PETS(A.I.P)'.

'A HAUNTED HOUSE'에서 정적은 급진적 행위가 된다—모든 포즈가 취약성과 반항 사이의 의도적 긴장 속에 얼어붙어, 현대적 우아함의 친밀한 초상을 만든다.
