THE SACRED UNBINDING

The analysis of the study of the corset, an ambivalent symbol of constraint and enhancement, constitutes the first research phase and opens the narrative journey by illustrating the evolution of a woman knight inspired by Giovanna D’Arco. The story explores emancipation from an imposed military role within a sexophobic and machismo-dominated society. The young female knight is alone throughout her journey, embodying a silent resistance and a determination that ultimately leads her to free herself from the constraints that limit her body and true identity. The story begins in a stable, a symbolic space of submission and humility, where the female rider wears armor—an emblem of rigidity and social imposition. Here, the armor and the splinted corset, layered together, represent a double constraint reflecting her inner struggle against patriarchal control and her desire for female self-determination. As the story progresses, the forest becomes a place of transition. In this space, the protagonist wears softer and lighter clothing, symbolizing freedom and rebirth. Within this natural setting, the rigidity of armor is abandoned in favor of flexibility, marking a loosening of social pressures and a reconnection with her true essence. In the concluding scene, the protagonist enters a deconsecrated church, a place of reflection, purification, and confession. She sheds all restraints, expressing through semi-nakedness a definitive and concrete liberation from the last symbolic constraints of control. The story uses costumes as symbols and metaphors to narrate the protagonist's evolution: from heavy armor to light clothing and leather, symbolizing strength, and finally to nudity. Her transformation reflects a journey of self-affirmation and rejection of sexophobic restrictions.
