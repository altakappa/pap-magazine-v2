VENUS

Photography + AI Collaboration Ekaterina Timko X Dustin Hollywood "Venus" is a project about a beautiful maiden who combines the divine and the demonic. About the divine harmony that joins in a magnificent dance with each other. The project explores the delicate balance between celestial beauty and mysterious darkness, conveying the essence of divine grace intertwined with a touch of the mysterious. Our Venus did not turn out in her classical interpretation as she is usually portrayed. Although the inspiration for this shoot was also Botticelli's paintings. Based on the model's appearance and personality, we created an image of divinity with a demonic beginning. As a result, the two opposites merged in a harmonious dance. The most mystical and beautiful work in this series “Venus Lucifer” The morning Venus, named "Bringing Light", is the harbinger of dawn. The Latin roots suggest a linguistic dance - "Lux" means light and "fero" means to bring, leading to "Lucifer", the bearer of light. Evening Venus was believed to have a divine, sublime face, while morning Venus was associated with lust, carnal pleasures, sex.
