BLUE BOOK

I create and execute the visions in my mind. The visions live in my daydreams. My artwork is inspired by my life growing up in the south. I tend to look through a warped lens recreating my childhood in an imaginative world reminiscent of Dr. Seuss. I find myself living in a fantasy, accompanied by beautiful figurines that are my friends and family. They loan themselves as models for the character I imagined and created through doing the hair, makeup, fashion design and tyling. My photos still exhibit bits and pieces from their everyday lives. They constantly share their version of the world around them. We act like children and don’t have any plans of changing that. I yearn to be a child again. I loved the carefree breathing and the constant excitement. When life is so hot it burns blue. When it comes to the clothes, or the scarcity of clothing in my imagery, I can’t give credit to couture clothing. I perceive clothing as everyday objects, because in reality that's what clothes are. Clothing is an ordinary pair of shoes or a t-shirt. It’s how you create the world around the pair of shoes and t-shirt that makes the clothing seem ethereal. I don’t have access to high fashion but everyday objects that I design and create mixed in with obtainable fashion creates my version of couture. My photography brings fashion back to the original state of creativity that contradicts the industry’s standard of labeled, couture, and high fashion clothing.

INSIGNIFICANCE OF HUMANS

In a higher dimensional space, there are such concepts as parallel universe-split and wormhole-distortion. When people or objects enter a higher dimensional space through wormholes, their forms will no longer be subject to control, thus giving rise to irregular patterns.  Firstly, Ziyu converted the concept and theory from lower dimensional space to higher dimensional space into visual images, then reduced the forms of higher dimensional space to the three-dimensional space where we are in, and presented them through the structure and silhouette of clothing.  Ziyu designed one-piece double-body tights to present the form that humans will split when they enter a higher dimensional space. Ziyu also designed deformed clothes that can be disassembled and reorganised through zippers and straps, so as to embody that the forms of humans or objects will change to some extent when they enter a higher dimensional space, but we will still be us.
