VIENTO NEGRO

“Black Wind” is a reflection on the era we are living in, and how it subtly, yet inevitably, affects us all. Wars, discrimination, and dehumanization create an atmosphere of constant tension, a "black wind" that slowly seeps into society, poisoning it and pushing it toward collective madness.
