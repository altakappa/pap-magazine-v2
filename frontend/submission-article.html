Before you write

What we publish

A piece that examines a phenomenon, a shift, an industry or an idea, and argues something about it. Reporting is welcome. Reviews and criticism are welcome. Brands and names may appear as evidence — that is normal.

Free to publish

Example: a piece on why quiet luxury ended, in which several labels appear along the way. The subject is the shift, not any one house.

Promotional pieces — 790 EUR

Example: a piece centred on one brand or artist, introducing a launch, a collection or an event. If the purpose of the text is to present that subject, it is a promotional piece.

The deciding question is not how many brands appear, but what the piece is for. Many brands with one being elevated is promotional. One brand examined critically is editorial. The final call is made by our editors during review — nothing is charged when you submit, and if your piece is not published you are charged nothing.

Requirements

  • English (800 words minimum) or Korean (1,600 characters minimum)
  • Plain text only — no HTML. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
  • One cover image, plus up to 9 images for the body. JPG, PNG or WebP, 25MB each.
  • You must hold the rights to every image you upload.
  • Cite your sources where you quote or use figures.
  • Review takes up to 7 business days.

Published also means social

Everything PAP publishes goes out on our official social channels as well — Instagram, Threads, X, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest. Submitting means you agree to that.
