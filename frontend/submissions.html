Submit Your Editorial to PAP Magazine

PAP Magazine is a Korean art-driven fashion, beauty and culture magazine published from Seoul, with a Milan desk. We accept fashion magazine submissions year-round from photographers, stylists and creative teams worldwide — no agency, no middleman, reviewed by our editorial team in 1–3 business days.

Every published editorial appears on the website in 9 languages with full team credits, and is credited to your Instagram handles on @pap_magazine (380K+ followers).

What We Publish

Fashion editorials, beauty stories and art-driven visual work. We look for a clear point of view: a moodboard-level concept carried through styling, casting, photography and post. Single images are welcome as part of a story — we publish editorials, not portfolios.

How to Submit

01

Create a free account — submissions are managed through your member page, where you can track review status in real time.

02

Complete the submission form — production info, team credits, look-by-look garment credits and high-resolution images. All content in English.

03

Free Publication Requirements

Publication is free when your editorial meets both conditions:

ConditionDetail
4+ looksEach look with at least one image and one credit (brand or Instagram).
4+ different clothing brandsGarment credits across your looks must span at least 4 different clothing brands. Generic credits (Stylist's Own, Vintage, Own Design) don't count toward the total.

Editorials below the threshold can still be submitted — they are reviewed as paid submissions (€380). Single-brand or brand-promotional editorials are classified as branded content (€790). Fees are charged only if your work is accepted; a declined submission costs nothing.

Technical Requirements

High-resolution JPG (longest side 2000px recommended, 1600×2000px (4:5)). All submission content — title, artist statement, team and model credits — written in English. Video (behind-the-scenes, clips) is shared as a download link and published on PAP's official YouTube channel.

What Published Contributors Get

A web editorial in 9 languages with full team credits · social posts crediting your team's Instagram handles · eligibility for Editor's Pick of the Month, featured on the homepage for a full month and promoted across PAP's channels. Premium members can additionally request one official PAP Pull Letter per month — a formal letter for pulling samples from brands and showrooms and negotiating locations.

FAQ

Is submitting to PAP Magazine free?
Yes — free for editorials with 4+ looks crediting 4+ different clothing brands. Below that threshold submissions are reviewed as paid (€380); single-brand editorials are branded content (€790). Fees apply only if accepted.
How long does the review take?
1–3 business days, with results by email. After approval, publication takes up to 2–3 weeks. You can track status in the MY SUBMISSIONS tab of your member page.
Do you accept submissions from outside Korea?
Yes. PAP works with creative teams worldwide — recent contributors span Europe, the Americas and Asia. All submission content must be in English for our global editorial team.
Can I submit work that was published elsewhere?
We prioritize unpublished editorials. If your work has appeared elsewhere, mention it in the artist statement so the editorial team can review it accordingly.
What is a Pull Letter?
An official letter issued in PAP's name that creative teams use to pull samples from brands and showrooms and to request production support. Issuance is selective and included in Premium membership (one request per month).
