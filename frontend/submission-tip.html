Before you send

What we are looking for

A moment worth reporting that you saw with your own eyes and recorded yourself — a public figure somewhere unexpected, an incident as it happened, something a lot of people would want to know about.

Only tips you witnessed and filmed yourself. We cannot use footage taken by someone else, and we do not accept rumours or second-hand claims — without something to verify there is nothing we can publish.

Your files are deleted after 7 days

Photos and videos you send are deleted 7 days after they arrive. If we use your tip, we keep a copy for the article before that happens. Nothing else is kept.

If we use it

You are credited in the article and you get one month of PAP Premium. There is no fee either way, and your identity is never published without your agreement.

What you can send

  • Video up to 1GB per file — MP4 or MOV
  • Photos up to 50MB each — JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC
  • Up to 10 files in one tip
  • Up to 5 tips a day per account

We check before we publish

Evidence is not the same as confirmation. We verify who is in the footage, when it was taken and what the context is, and our editors decide whether it can be published at all. Sending a tip does not mean it will run.
